01 — Meri Galti

Maine galat kiya.

Maine gusse mein tumhe ulta-seedha bola. Jo maine kaha, woh galat tha.

Main ye justify nahi karunga ki main gusse mein tha, kyunki gussa meri galti ko sahi nahi bana deta.

Mujhe samajhna chahiye tha ki mere words tumhe kitna hurt kar sakte hain. Mujhe rukna chahiye tha, sochna chahiye tha aur phir baat karni chahiye thi.

“Sorry sirf ek word nahi hai. Galti samajhna aur usse repeat na karna bhi uska part hai.”
02 — I'm Sorry
Hemadri

I'm genuinely sorry. Tumhe hurt karna mera intention nahi tha, lekin meri baaton se tum hurt hui — aur uski responsibility meri hai.

03 — Our Memories

Good moments deserve a place too ❤️

Tumhare saath bitaya har achha moment special hai.
I don't want one bad moment to erase all the good ones.
04 — Main Kya Chahta Hu

No pressure.

Agar tumhe thoda time chahiye, main samajhunga. Tumhari feelings aur tumhari space important hai.

✓

Main apni galti ko excuse nahi banaunga.

✓

Main tumhe reply ke liye force nahi karunga.

✓

Bas itna chahta hu ki tumhe pata ho ki mujhe apni galti ka genuinely regret hai.
